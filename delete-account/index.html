Delete Your Account

Bandpath IELTS (Bandpath IELTS Prep) — Android package com.ieltsprep.app

You can permanently delete your Bandpath IELTS account and all of your account data at any time, directly from within the app. This page explains exactly how, what gets deleted, and what doesn't.

1. How to delete your account in the app

Deletion is available from two places in the app — both trigger the same deletion:

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap the red Delete account row (trash icon), near the bottom of the screen. It's also reachable from Help & support on the same Profile screen.
  4. A confirmation dialog titled "Delete account" appears, stating: "This permanently deletes your profile, progress, and test history. This cannot be undone." Tap Delete account again to confirm.
  5. The app deletes your account and data, signs you out, and returns you to the sign-in screen.

Deletion happens immediately once you confirm — there is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone or reversed after confirming.

2. If you can't sign in to the app

If you're locked out of your account and can't complete the in-app steps above, email vinnakotabunny760@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and request deletion. We will verify the request and delete your account manually.

3. What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your authentication record and, because every one of the tables below is linked to it by a foreign key that cascades on deletion, every row tied to your account across our entire backend, including:

  • Your profile (name, email, IELTS goal, target band, exam date, weakest skill, study preferences)
  • Your subscription/entitlement status
  • Every Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking, and Full Mock attempt and score
  • Your submitted Writing essays, Speaking transcripts, and the AI feedback generated for them
  • Your AI Coach chat history
  • Your vocabulary/grammar progress, bookmarks, achievements, study streak, and XP
  • Your daily AI-usage log entries
  • Any pending RevenueCat webhook records tied to your account, which are removed as a separate explicit step before the account itself is deleted

This is a complete, unconditional deletion — our backend has no "soft delete," archive, or recovery mechanism for a deleted account. Once confirmed, it cannot be undone.

4. What is not deleted by this

Deleting your Bandpath IELTS account does not reach data held by independent third parties outside our system:

  • Google Play keeps its own transaction and billing history for any purchase you made, under Google's own retention policy — this is Google's record of the purchase, not something stored in our database, and we have no ability to delete it.
  • RevenueCat (our subscription-status provider) and any AI provider used to generate a response to a request you already sent (see our Privacy Policy) each retain data only under their own respective policies, not ours.

We do not have a separate retention period of our own for any account data — our systems do not keep a copy of anything listed in Section 3 after deletion completes.

5. Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription

Important: if you have an active Premium subscription, deleting your Bandpath IELTS account does not cancel it or stop future billing. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are handled by two separate systems.

To stop being charged, cancel your subscription directly in Google Play, either:

  • In the app: go to the Subscription screen and tap Manage subscription, which opens Google Play's own subscription management page for this app, or
  • Directly: open https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions?package=com.ieltsprep.app and cancel from there.

We recommend cancelling your subscription in Google Play before or after deleting your account — either order works, but deleting the account alone will not stop billing.

6. Questions

For anything else about account deletion or your data, email vinnakotabunny760@gmail.com, or see our Privacy Policy.
